Career Intelligence is a personal job-search project planned as a dedicated workspace inside Second Brain. Its goal is to help one candidate discover and organise relevant software-development opportunities from permitted sources.

Status: In developmentPersonal useIndia-focused job search

Project purpose

Job opportunities can arrive through several job boards, alert emails and public feeds. This project is intended to bring permitted information into one private workspace so that opportunities can be assessed consistently.

01 / DISCOVERY

Find relevant opportunities

Bring together authorised job-alert messages and permitted job-source feeds, with an emphasis on entry-level software roles in India.

02 / ORGANISATION

Reduce duplicate noise

Plan to identify repeated vacancy listings while retaining the original source and job link for review.

03 / EVALUATION

Make fit understandable

Plan to compare role requirements with a candidate profile and keep uncertain experience, location and compensation details clearly marked.

Planned scope

The product specification has been prepared and its initial screen concepts approved. The following capabilities are planned; they are not yet implemented.

Discovery and job review

  • Frontend, React.js, Backend, Node.js and entry-level software roles
  • Gujarat first, then other Indian cities
  • Remote roles only where India eligibility is supported by evidence
  • Permanent full-time and paid trainee opportunities
  • Evidence-based experience, freshness and source-quality assessment

Personal application organisation

  • Separate personal bookmarks from submitted applications
  • Track employer progression only when reliable evidence exists
  • Keep unknown compensation and unclear eligibility as unknown
  • Preserve original source references for review
  • Keep the candidate in control of applications and communications

Permission and privacy principles

Permitted access only

The project is intended to use authorised APIs, public feeds and separately authorised email access. It is not intended to scrape logged-in job-board accounts or automate account access with passwords.

Candidate control

Discovering a job does not submit an application. Saving a job does not mean an employer has shortlisted the candidate. Personal profile or resume information is not sent to job sources merely to discover vacancies.
